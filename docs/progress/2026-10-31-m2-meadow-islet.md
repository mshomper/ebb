# B3a: the meadow islet and its sandbar (m2-meadow-islet)

Status: Done

The Tier 1 milestone (the second happy pond species) earns the meadow islet, an oblong about 60 m by 34 m lying along the coast, its middle 86 m from the island's, in the open sea on the side away from the camp and the spit, placed by the seed (`recipes/land/meadow-islet.json`, `src/world/islet`). It first shows at the big low tide of day 27 at 18:00, or the first low water after a later milestone: a pale shoal grows out of the seabed, then over one tide cycle it rises to sit just above high tide, with a wandering sandbar to the shore that is dry only around low water. The rise writes only the islet's own cells; its chunks are drawn, and the water, the walk grid and the view above grow to hold it. A risen islet stays risen through saves (the `islet` section; older saves load without it), shows in the reveal if it rose while you were away, and gets a journal note, a morning hint and a thread in the Keeper book. Its biome is machair (Matt, October 31, 2026): mostly sand with sparse grassy patches, and five plants of its own, planted when it rises and found nowhere on the pond island (they never volunteer and are not in the Plant tool): harebell, bird's-foot trefoil and wild thyme on the open sand, red clover and lady's bedstraw on the grassy patches, each with a flora recipe, journal page, web ties, field sketch and drawn icon. Thyme, clover and trefoil grow as mats (Matt, October 31, 2026): cover plants that spread square by square from their planted clumps, each as it does in the wild (thyme creeps and roots slowly over the driest sand, trefoil flings seed into many small mats, clover fills the grassy patches into wide beds for the bees), ragged at the edges, with open ground between cushions, resting in winter and never crowding one another; they are drawn as stacked shells, low carpets with flower heads on top near the eye and soft drifts of their flowers' color from above, with clover's round heads standing on stalks (`src/render/passes/mats.js`, `mat-shells.wgsl`; `tools/mat-growth-sim.mjs` projects a year of spread). Flax has its slot for wave 2. Other systems read the store slice `islet` (`phase`, `risen`, `riseDay`, `bar`, `box`). Test links: `?islet`, `?islet=rising`, `?islet=0.5`, `?islet=earned`.
