# B3 S0: visits and shaped ground (m2-visits-shaped)

Status: Done

What every B3 newcomer stands on, built once before the creatures. The island now measures the homes B3's seasonal newcomers want from its own heights and paint: a raised bar in the tide band (its size, sand and whether a walker came near in the last day, plus the meadow islet's bar when it has risen), a Terrace sea wall at the waterline (the island's own rocky point never counts), each dug pond with its age, distance to the sea, wet grass between and reeds round its margin, and wrack left lying on the strandline. The coast is shared out, so a wall shows the shore it took. Every check reports numbers; a species says which it wants in its recipe.

A seasonal newcomer visits first: on days its seed picks, in its season, it shows itself (the species' own module draws it, from `visit:start`), and the next morning a hint note comes, ringing where it showed itself. A visit while the island was away still leaves the hint. Visits stop once its home is made. Species plug in with recipe lines only (docs/11 "Visits and shaped homes"). Test links `?shape=bar,wall,pond,wrack` and `?visit=<sp>`; `tools/shaped-lab.html` shows each check on the starter island.
