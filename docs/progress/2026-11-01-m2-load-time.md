# A faster start, with a loading screen (m2-load-time)

Status: Done

The island is ready in about 7 seconds instead of 16. Most of the old wait went to sculpting the small earth heaps beside dug holes: the same three shapes were sculpted once for each of the six soil colours, at a detail finer than anyone can see. Now each shape is sculpted once, at 8 mm detail, and coloured per soil. The heaps' surface stays within 3 mm of the old one, with a quarter of the points.

While the island is made, a loading screen shows the wordmark on the dusk colour, with one quiet line underneath that follows the start (raising the island, filling the sea, waking the creatures, laying out the shore, setting out the camp, lighting the sky). It fades out when the island shows, and goes at once if the start ends with a card instead (no WebGPU, the save open in another window). The lines are in `recipes/world/loading.json`.
