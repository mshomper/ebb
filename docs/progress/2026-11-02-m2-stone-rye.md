# F20: the marker stone and the rye (m2-stone-rye)

Status: Done

The meadow islet now comes up with an old standing stone on its dome, lichen-crusted, leaning a little, with a V notch cut in its top, and a few rye stalks gone wild at its foot. An islet that rose before this build gets them on its next load. Gathering the wild heads puts rye seed in the seed tin, and the wild stalks come back every late summer and autumn, so the rye never runs out. One handful of rye seed sows a strip about 2 by 6 m on the islet's grass. Seed sown outside autumn waits in the ground. The rye comes up green in late autumn, rests through winter and stands in gold ear by late summer. With the sickle, Cut the rye on a ripe stalk's card cuts what is in reach, so a strip takes a few swings, and stands it in stooks. They dry for three game days, longer after rain, and Take up gives rye straw (a new pack kind) and rye seed.

At noon on summer day 0, in a year after the islet rose, the game's own sun through the notch puts a pale patch of light on the ground about a meter north of the stone. It stays until six the next morning. A dig there turns up an old pot with the sickle blade inside. That opens the sickle at the workbench (blade, stick, rush cord, a set no other make uses). From then on the stone's card tells the season by its noon shadow.
