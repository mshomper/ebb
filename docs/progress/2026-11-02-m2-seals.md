# Grey seals and the pup (claude/m2-seals-63b031)

Status: Done

B3 wave 3's grey seals. Matt's model (the body cut to 6,000 triangles with his go, eyes and jaw as he made them) is rigged in code with eleven bones and plays its clips: lying curved like a banana, breathing, looking about, scratching, yawning, humping along the sand, swimming with the hind flippers and bottling upright in the water. The coat is painted in the shader: mottled grey, a darker back, a paler belly, darker and glossier while wet. They visit offshore in autumn and winter, then haul out on a quiet sandbar you raised (or the meadow islet's bar while you keep off it), slip into the sea when you come too close at Wary, and leave a bar people keep walking on. A white pup comes on a seeded day of 29 to 35, lies and nurses by its mother, waves and rolls, moults from day 14 and goes to sea after 21 days. Watching only, up to Curious; at 0.5 one swims in and bottles near you. Journal page, web ties, morning hints, sketchbook subject, a drawn icon, soft code-made sounds, test links and `tools/seal-lab.html`.
