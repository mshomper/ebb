# The underground spring: ore, the clay furnace, the way down and the first zone (m2-underground-spring)

Status: Done

The spring-ore plan, Matt, October 30 to 31, 2026. **O1:** iron ore, copper ore, iron, copper, nails, twisted sticks, planks and deep clay as pack kinds with drawn icons; the clay furnace, a camp build in the work corner from deep clay, sand and marram, dries a night, then smelts ore with willow charcoal overnight and draws metal into nails; twisted sticks and planks at the workbench. **O2:** the cairn trail ends just above the spring's head; the letter ends "Dig above the spring, where the water starts. Go gently."; a worn mark, and the first spadeful there gives way into a hole that never fills, ringed with boulders, with Climb down on its card. **O3:** zones (doc 26): a fade, the swap to the zone's own scene, light, sound and walk grid, the island simulating on; the cave round the pool, seen from inside, lit by the hole; the old keeper's pan, a few pans a day of iron or copper ore. **Small lights:** up to eight, the most important to the view, fading in and out (`?lanterns=12`). **O4, the pan finds:** ochre (ochre slip fires a pot red) and mica (the mica lantern), keepsakes (rock crystal, crinoid beads, garnet sand, a cave pearl, a fleck of gold once ever), the old keeper's thimble, button and key on the 12th, 24th and 36th pans, and the well shrimp, a journal find. `?spring`, `?spring=cave`, `?dig=spring`; `tools/unit-tests-ore.js`, `tools/unit-tests-small-lights.js`
