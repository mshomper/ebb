# B3 wave 3: the teal and the teal hat (m2-teal)

Status: Done

A small winter flock comes to a reedy pond you dug, once the frogs are asleep, and leaves in early spring. Before their home exists, a few birds drop onto a pond in winter, dabble for two hours and rise off it with a whirr, and the next morning a note hints at what would keep them. Once a dug pond of at least 30 m² has reeds, rushes or iris along 60% of its edge, a flock of 3 to 8 settles (one bird for every 5 m² of pond). If the pond loses its reeds or its water they grow uneasy and leave after 3 days. The hourly tick runs them on the turnstones' flock arc with the pond as their pool, seeded and safe through catch-up; nothing changes on an island without a home.

Each bird is Matt's teal model (the 1,200-triangle flock file, not cut further) skinned in code to R1's small-bird rig: legs to their own side, the spine blended from tail to head, and each flank's wing copied and skinned to the arm and hand so it opens in flight. Drawn at 2 times real (0.72 m; Matt, November 2, 2026: 2.5 looked giant beside the walker). On the water they swim with paddling feet, dabble, up-end until only the tail and kicking feet show, preen and float; at night most sleep with their heads on their backs. Only the walker startles them. At Wary they spring straight up off the water one after another, circle the pond fast and low on whirring wings, and land on the far side; past Wary they swim off instead. Trust comes from watching only and stops at Curious. At 0.5 comes their group moment: they swim in around you and leave their green wing feather, into a pouch (or on the gift stone once that stands). Feather, linen cloth and rush cord make the teal hat at the workbench: a second hat in the kit's hat slot, worn at once, with the green feather in its band. While you wade in it, creatures' comfort rings shrink to 70%. Their voice is made in code: the drake's ringing krik, a quiet murmur at rest, the whirr of a flush.
