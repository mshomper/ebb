# The Hollow moves, cinematic pass (claude/hollow-moves-cine)

Status: Done

The twelve rank 1 to 3 Hollow moves and the staff's swing now gather, land and answer, in the move arena only (`tools/move-arena.html?course=hollow`). The camera takes part in each move: it eases in or pulls back on the wind-up, kicks on release, shakes in proportion to the hit, and for Groundline, Kindling burst and Cast the flame's landing it slides toward the effect and settles back. A Camera motion slider turns all of that down, or off. Hits hold the fight still for a few frames, and the big peaks slow it for a moment. This bends only the fight's step and what is drawn, never world time. The lantern dims while power gathers, flashes as the move leaves (a local flash, bright at the blow and falling off, that throws soft shadows of the walker, the things and the pillars while the room's corners stay dark), and leaves an afterglow on the floor. Embers arc, bounce and lie glowing as they cool. Dust is a lit puff, not a glow. Scorches and cracks stay a few seconds. Each beat sends a sound cue (`hollow:beat`), with no audio yet. The swing's old filled fan on the floor is now a slash, a streak along the staff tip's path at staff height that is gone in 0.2 s, and its contact throws chips and a star big enough to read at 5.5 m. Clips are retimed, not replaced: a slow rise, a held top, the release exactly as the wind-up ends, then a follow-through. Wind-ups are longer (Groundline 0.45 s, Kindling burst 0.55 s, Cast the flame 0.32 s), and a roll in a wind-up cancels the move with nothing spent.

The swing and every targeted move now reach things up to 1.8 m over the floor. Locked on, a move aims at the thing's body, so Cast the flame bursts on the floating watcher at 1.5 m and its embers rain down, and the chest leans back toward a floater. The floor dot is redrawn as a small ink ring on a soft halo, every point lifted over the floor or the water under it, so the pool and the floor's slopes no longer clip through it. The Hollow camera already used the doc's numbers: 5.5 m on the slant to a point 1.3 m up, 32 degrees, which is 4.7 m behind on the floor. Doc 16 now says so. Numbers live in `recipes/world/hollow-cine.json`, with sliders in the arena's "How the moves feel" panel. New tests: `unit-tests-hollow-cine.js`, plus `unit-tests-clip-knots.js`, which proves every existing clip plays exactly as before.
