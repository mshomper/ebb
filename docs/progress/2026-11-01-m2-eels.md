# B3: the eels (m2-eels)

Status: Done

European eels come to a pond you dug long ago near the sea. Before that home exists, now and then on an autumn night one comes in from the sea, noses up the wet shore and slips back, and the next morning a hint says what it was looking for. Once the pond is a year old (56 game days), within 30 m of the sea with wet grass between, the first eels come on a rainy autumn night at 22:00: if you are watching, they come up out of the sea on the nearest shore and cross the wet grass to the pond, one after another. More come on later rainy autumn nights, up to what the pond holds. By night they hunt low along the pond floor; by day they lie under the weed with only a slow ripple down the tail; a walker coming fast sends them down to the deep spot. They sleep in the pond mud through the winter.

The eel is built in code: a long body on a chain of 14 bones, posed as one wave along the chain (swimming, the wave slips back and grows toward the tail; crossing grass, it stays on the ground while the body slides through it). Watching only (trust up to Tolerant): no treats, no name, no gift, and they never count toward a tier milestone. A journal page, a drawn icon, web ties, a morning hint, a sketchbook subject and a winter sleep window. Arrival runs in the hourly tick from the seed and the weather (a gate on the swarm model: autumn, a day with rain, the home made), so time away gives the same eels.
