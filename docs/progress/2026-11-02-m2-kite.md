# F20: the kite (m2-kite)

Status: Done

The kite from F20's stories without creatures. On the first windy autumn day with the wind blowing onto the bar after the meadow islet rises, a torn kite lies on the islet's strandline (seeded from the weather's day records, so an island left alone finds it there too); a morning hint mentions it, and Take it up puts it in a pouch. At the workbench the torn kite, rush cord and a stick mend it (a set no other make uses). The mended kite is a tool on the Keeper book's belt and hangs on the tent's back pole at camp. Fly is on the kite's own card, never on the action bar (Matt, November 1, 2026): offered when you stand on the islet's top in wind, and anywhere else the card says where and when it flies. While it flies, Rise with it on the same card lifts the view, slow and high, to hold the island, the bar, the islet and the stack offshore in one frame; any key or click brings it back, and walking off reels it in. Each high view adds a page to the sketchbook ("Kite", for Matt's drawings kite-1 to kite-3). The kite thread has three lines. Test links: `?islet&kite`, `?islet&kite=mended`, `?islet&kite=fly`.
