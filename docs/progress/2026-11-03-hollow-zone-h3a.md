# The Hollow as a zone: a trip down and back (claude/hollow-zone-h3a-28870d)

Status: Done

With the driftwood staff made and the old lantern on its hook, the arch in the stack's mouth offers Go through. A trip begins: the screen goes dark while the first floor is built in the workers from H1's floor maker, seeded by the island, the trip's number and the floor's place. You land in the way in, under a rope from the roof (the way up). Somewhere ahead is the way down, a shaft in the floor with a rope staked beside it. Its card says what is left in the lantern in words ("Half a light left in the lantern") and offers Go down. Climb up, or Go up, brings you back by the hook keeping everything. The floors are empty of things for now; H3b brings the fighting.

The walker's lantern hangs from the left hand and is the only light below: a real point light, its shadows soft-traced through the floor's own signed field (uploaded as a 3D texture, 3 to 5 MB a floor), with occlusion and a faint bounce. It is fuel and health in one. A fill lasts about 18 minutes on the first floor and burns 10% faster each floor down, about 4.8 floors at 3 minutes a floor. A drawn flame on the HUD is the only gauge. Below a quarter the light shrinks and the walker says "The light won't last." When it goes out, the flame gutters, the screen holds dark while you feel your way up, and you wake by the hook, low on the floor, then kneel and get up; a seeded third of the trip's finds is dropped (the list is empty until H3c). Walls between the camera and you are cut away and ghosted back with a soft outline, so the Hollow camera (4 to 7 m back, tilt 20 to 55 degrees) never pulls in. At Matt's ask the floors take the stack mouth's treatment (decision 0001): flagstones, bedding ledges, joints and rocky bulges in the field, and the island's rock scan on the rock. The trip count and deepest floor are saved in a new `hollow` section; being below is never saved. Test links: `?hollow`, `?hollow=3`, `?hollow=dark`.
