# B3 R1: the small-bird rig and the turnstone (m2-bird-rig)

Status: Done

A reusable rig for small birds, written against fifteen bone roles (body, neck, head, tail, a two-part wing a side, a two-bone leg and toes a side), with every proportion in a recipe (`recipes/birds/turnstone.json`), so the purple sandpiper and later birds are a new shape and plumage, not new code. The legs reach for where the gait puts each foot, timed to the distance walked, so a foot on the sand never slides; the wing telescopes from folded along the flank to a flat span. The ruddy turnstone is made in code on it (`src/world/creatures/bird-model.js`, `assets/models/turnstone/CREDITS.md`), in winter and breeding plumage, drawn at 0.64 m. It is baked fine and then cut by quadric edge collapse (now in `src/world/mesh/decimate.js`, colour and skin weights riding along) to 3,000 triangles, or 1,200 for a bird in a flock, in line with the other creatures (the hen is about 1,500). Its clips: breathing and looking about, the quick scurry and run, pecking, turning a stone or a clump of wrack with its bill, preening, take-off, flight with a glide now and then (a flock flies each bird on its own beat), and landing with the wings held high. `tools/shore-birds-lab.html` shows each clip and a loop of its day. The species, its journal page, visits and homes come in wave 2.
