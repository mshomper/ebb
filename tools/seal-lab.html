ebb seal lab

The grey seal (B3 wave 3): Matt's model, the body cut to about 6,000 triangles, rigged in code with eleven bones (src/world/seals/model.js), playing its clips (motion.js). The coat here is a quick sketch of the game's (seal-draw.wgsl paints the real one, with its blotches). The grid is 10 cm, darker every metre; the blue band is the water line in the swimming and bottling clips. Drag to turn the view, scroll to come closer. All tools
