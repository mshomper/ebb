ebb tools

Local test pages. They use the same code as the game. Back to the island

  • Slice sign-offThe vertical slice's eight checks, each with steps, what you should see and a test link. Mark each Pass or Not yet, then copy the results to paste to Claude.
  • Frame timingThe island running live, with a graph of how long each drawing step takes on the graphics card.
  • Cliff benchWhat the Cliff tool's full detail near cliff faces costs on each quality tier: builds a cliff scene, then measures the terrain and shadow passes with that detail on and off, high, medium and low in turn.
  • Layout checkThe game in eight narrow and short window sizes, one after another, each checked for sideways scrolling, buttons under 44 px, text under 15 px and buttons on top of each other.
  • Water labThe island with its live water, showing volume, wet area, fastest flow, broken cells and cost, with buttons that sculpt near the spring and pond to test it.
  • Shore labThe island you can shape and paint, with a map of the shore zones it works out from the tide (kelp, wrack, lichen, wet sand, mud) and a sea-level slider showing what each tide uncovers. "Show sheltered shore" warms the coves that catch the most keepsakes.
  • Walker figureYou on foot, rigged and moving: pace back and forth at a creep, a stroll or brisk on flat ground, a slope or a shelf, wade, turn on the spot, sit, kneel, feed, go down and up, and try every expression of the eyes. Drag to turn it.
  • Footstep picksCC0 footstep recordings for the walker, one group per ground (grass, sand, mud, rock, water, pebbles), to listen to and keep or set aside.
  • Walk labThe island from above, coloured by where the walker can stand at the tide you pick, with the test sandbar and islet. Tap to stand, tap to walk, double tap to hurry; move the tide to watch the bar open and close.
  • Find modelsThe shore finds' models at true scale side by side: the anemone open and shut, the crabs (the hermit crab walking) and the shells. Drag one to turn it.
  • Find sketchesEvery find's journal sketch in pencil, ink and wash, beside some plant sketches to compare.
  • Keepsake labEvery keepsake family the sea leaves on the strandline, at each rarity from common to once in a lifetime, with its shelf sketch and paper, and a table of how often each comes at each tide.
  • Catch labThe living things the nets bring up, moving as they do on the tray or as their journal sketch, and what thousands of hauls hold at each tide, season and depth.
  • Artifact variantsYour nine artifacts morphed, recoloured and named from their seeds: 48 at a time, the way the nets will bring them up.
  • Artifact modelsThe nine baked artifact models as they are, before any variation, each with its credit.
  • Journal reviewEvery plant's journal page side by side: sketch and icon, then each part of the page lined up across the plants, with empty parts and much longer or shorter wording flagged at the top.
  • Growth labRuns 14 game days of growth on a copy of the island, with charts of reed clumps and grass cover, a check that two runs end the same, and a habitat map with the clumps on it.
  • Ecology labFrogs arriving on the starter island and Two ponds side by side: when the pond became right, the sign, settling and each frog that came or left over the game days, frogs against the room the edge has, the midges, maps of each need, and a second run that must end the same.
  • Turtle labThe pond turtle at its drawn size, every motion side by side and looping: the swim stroke, the periscope, walking, hauling out, basking and drying, slipping off with a plop, grazing, peeking. Close on the head, slow motion, and the shell before and after its plates were drawn per pixel.
  • Frog labThe island with frogs on it at any time of day: the chorus call by call with the gap rule checked, hops and swims, and what moving and drawing the frogs costs.
  • Creature studioThe frog, the minnow or the southern hawker on a stage, baked and posed as the game does it and moving with its own motion (play, pause, slow motion), with sliders for its colors, part sizes, drawn size and motion. Save to repo writes only the numbers you moved into that species' recipe file.
  • Sketch labEvery journal field sketch, at the pencil, ink or washed stage, with all its labels; tap one to see it large.
  • Music labThe piano and the support kit, single pieces with their score, the director's day simulated (Day tab: Pool, The game shows what the island plays), the four tides in every season, and the composer growing new pieces (Engine tab).
  • Music renderPick a season, time of day and seed (or a piece by name from the list), listen to it, and download it as a WAV file: rendered through the game's music sound, silence trimmed, loudness set to -18 LUFS.
  • Music tuningHow often music plays, what every number in recipes/music/director.json does, how to check a change, and how to save it with GitHub Desktop.
  • Audio checkThe island's sounds and the frogs driven by sliders: every bed and small sound by season, hour, weather, tide and camera height, with a pond to walk around.
  • Sound picks110 CC0 field recordings from Freesound for the island's beds and small sounds, to listen to with headphones and mark.
  • Audio testsQuick checks of the music engine, the director, the composer, the sampler and the game's sound hook-up.
  • Sky labThe island at any hour and season you pick, with the sun and moon positions, day length and exposure, without moving the world clock.
  • Clock dialThe clock dial from the island's top left, drawn large through a day and across the seasons, with the line it says under each one.
  • Palette labTune a palette on the island with a before and after divider, an eyedropper and sliders, then copy or download the palette file.
  • Texture labDownloads the ground photo scans, matches their colors to the recipe, previews them tiled under a low sun and saves the packed texture files into the repo.
  • Rock labPainted rock on flat ground, side by side: today's tiling against other tilings, or other rock scans from Poly Haven (add ?scans= and their names).
  • DeterminismBuilds the starter island three ways for a seed and shows that every run gives the same island, with a map of it from above.
  • Catch-up benchChecks that time away gives the same island as time watched, in pieces or at once, in a worker or on the page and through a save, then times 7 game days (the cap) of catch-up hour by hour against the budget.
  • Save upgradesOpens every old sample save in tools/save-samples with this build, brings it up to today's save format and checks its world fingerprint; you can also check one .ebb file from disk.
  • Terrain brush benchTimes island building, brush strokes and the settling step after a stroke, against the budgets in docs/08.
  • Unit testsEvery unit test in one run: the core, terrain, water, growth, the journal, creatures, keepsakes and the shelf.
