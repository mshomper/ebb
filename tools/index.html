ebb tools

Local test pages. They use the same code as the game. Back to the island

  • Frame timingThe island running live, with a graph of how long each drawing step takes on the graphics card.
  • Water labThe island with its live water, showing volume, wet area, fastest flow, broken cells and cost, with buttons that sculpt near the spring and pond to test it.
  • Growth labRuns 14 game days of growth on a copy of the island, with charts of reed clumps and grass cover, a check that two runs end the same, and a habitat map with the clumps on it.
  • Sky labThe island at any hour and season you pick, with the sun and moon positions, day length and exposure, without moving the world clock.
  • Palette labTune a palette on the island with a before and after divider, an eyedropper and sliders, then copy or download the palette file.
  • Texture labDownloads the ground photo scans, matches their colors to the recipe, previews them tiled under a low sun and saves the packed texture files into the repo.
  • DeterminismBuilds the starter island three ways for a seed and shows that every run gives the same island, with a map of it from above.
  • Terrain brush benchTimes island building, brush strokes and the settling step after a stroke, against the budgets in docs/08.
  • Unit testsQuick checks that random numbers, the clock, events, the store and the worker pool behave.
