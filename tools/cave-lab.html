Cave lab: the Hollow's floor maker

H1. Each floor is planned from the island seed, the trip, the floor and its level, dressed in its depth's chamber shapes, sampled as a signed field across every core, meshed by surface nets in a worker, and walked from the way in to the way down. Nothing here is in the game yet. Numbers live in recipes/world/hollow.json (floors).

Why surface nets and not dual contouring: surface nets was already in the repo (the creature bodies), is fast, and its slightly rounded edges suit worn rock. Dual contouring would give the glass halls sharper facet edges and crystal tips, at roughly two to three times the meshing time and with care needed at thin features. That is worth trying only if the glass halls read soft once H3 lights them.

Make floors

Maps: the walk grid in 0.5 m cells under the chamber graph. Field: the signed distance, air in teal and rock in stone, banded every half metre, the rock face as a dark line; level is 1 m over each chamber's floor; upright follows the main path from the way in to the way down, heights drawn twice as tall, with the walk grid's floor in orange. 3D: drag to turn, wheel to come closer, and lower the roof cut to look in from above.
