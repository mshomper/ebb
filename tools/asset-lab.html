Which way it faces

The orange arrow on the ground points along +Z. The model's front should face it.
The thin blue line points along +X.

A model that faces the wrong way is fixed by its yawDeg in tools/assets/models.json and a re-run of the build. Front toward the blue line (+X): -90. Front away from the blue line (-X): 90. Front away from the arrow (-Z): 180. "Look at the front" stops the turning and looks back along the arrow.

Color map difference

08162432+

The original's color map is drawn down to the copy's size and compared pixel by pixel, in 0 to 255 units. Grey is the copy's own picture where nothing changed; amber to red marks change. A mean under about 3 is good: the painting carried over. Higher, or red patches, means the paint moved or blurred there.
