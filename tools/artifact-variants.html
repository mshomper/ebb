Artifact variants

Every keepsake artifact is one of nine baked models (assets/models/keepsakes/) pushed and pulled by seeded numbers: stretched, tapered, twisted, bent, dented, worn smooth, chipped or broken, recoloured, banded, stained, crusted and sometimes gilded or paired with a twin. The numbers are continuous, so the variety does not run out, and each variant names and describes itself from them. Rarer ones may go further and carry marks. Each square is drawn as the shelf would draw it (an ink sketch traced from its mesh, washed in its own colours) on the paper its rarity gets. Tap one to see it close, turn it and move its numbers by hand. The numbers live in recipes/world/keepsake-artifacts.json.
