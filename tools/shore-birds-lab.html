ebb shore birds lab

The small-bird rig (B3 R1) carrying the ruddy turnstone, made in code from recipes/birds/turnstone.json and drawn at the findable scale (about 0.64 m, 2.8 times real). Life runs its whole day on a loop: a scurry, turning a stone, pecks, a run, preening, take-off, a lap in the air and a landing back where it began. Each clip plays on its own too. The grid is 10 cm (darker every metre), so a foot that slid would show. Drag to turn the view, scroll to come closer. All tools
