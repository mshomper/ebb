Each species is one region of a body-plan space; every card is a unique individual inside it. Tap a fish to see it large, its traits, and the growth grid that shapes it. Spots and maze patterns are grown by reaction–diffusion on the fish's own body grid, so they stretch and crowd toward the head and tail the way real markings do. Fish in a set are drawn to true scale against each other (the largest fills its card), with a scale bar. Rare morphs are boosted to about 1 in 7 here so you can see them while testing. The four rare types turn up on their own about 1 in 20 species in their waters; tap one under Rare types to see it directly. Each rare type has three species, each with its own conditions; about 4 in 10 ordinary species also have one.
