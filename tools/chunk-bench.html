Notes

  • Both are the real islands. Today's grows from recipes/land/starter-island.json (coast radius 29.5 m), the larger from recipes/land/starter-island-2.json (36 m); each land recipe gives its own chunk range, center and camera reach.
  • In the frame, a new island grows from the larger island's land, so for today's island the bench points the starter island's entry in recipes/world/terrain.json at starter-island.json, in memory only.
  • Chunks are whole 32 m squares centered on multiples of 32 m, so the larger island's 4 x 4 block opens from -80 to 48 m and the island is centered at (-16, -16).
  • The water sim's flux and depth steps run in one compute pass, so they are timed together, every step of a frame included.
  • Wetness runs now and then on game time, not every frame: its bar is the time of one run.
  • Chrome rounds GPU timestamps coarsely (to about 0.1 ms) unless chrome://flags "WebGPU Developer Features" is on. Turn it on before trusting the small passes: with it off, a pass under 0.1 ms often reads as 0.
  • Without timestamp-query only the whole frame can be timed (submit to work done, on the CPU), and the pass rows stay empty.
