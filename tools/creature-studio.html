ebb creature studio

The common frog on a paused stage, baked and posed exactly as the game does it. Move a slider and the frog changes at once. A changed slider gets an orange dot. Your changes stay in this browser until you save them to the recipe file (only the numbers you moved change) or undo them. Drag the frog to turn it, scroll to come closer. The grid is 1 cm, darker every 5 cm, at real size. All tools
