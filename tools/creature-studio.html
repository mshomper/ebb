ebb creature studio

One creature on a stage, baked and posed exactly as the game does it, moving as it does in the game. Pick a species, then move a slider and it changes at once. A changed slider gets an orange dot. Your changes stay in this browser, for each species, until you save them to its recipe file (only the numbers you moved change) or undo them. Drag the creature to turn it, scroll or pinch to come closer. The grid is 1 cm, darker every 5 cm, at real size. All tools
