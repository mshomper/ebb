      Your sketches

      Tap any fish to sketch or unsketch it, in any order.

      Each sketch inks part of the chart: its water's coast, depth lines and marks, and the fish itself. A finished water gets its name in ink and a note on what bites there. The compass inks by quarters. About every second fish sketched inks one riddle for a rare fish, with its numbered mark on the chart, easiest first. The last riddle and the chart's title ink with the final sketch. In the game there's no count; the chart itself shows how far along it is.
