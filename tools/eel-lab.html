Eel lab

The eels' spine-chain rig and live motion from above (src/world/creatures/eel.js, eel-swim.js), with the numbers in recipes/species/eel.json. The pond is on the left, wet grass in the middle, the sea on the right. Each dot is a bone. Click the pond to send a walker past and startle them.

Swimming, the wave runs back faster than the eel moves (it slips through the water) and grows toward the tail. Crossing the grass, the wave stays put on the ground and the body slides through it, so it follows its head's track. Resting, only a slow ripple runs down the tail.

In the game the eels are drawn 1.6 times their real 45 cm, so they can be found under the water. This page draws them at that size.
