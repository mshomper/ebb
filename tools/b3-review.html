The B3 check

The last step of M2 (doc 20, stage B3): the meadow islet, the autumn and winter visitors, the light on the stack and the islet's stories. Walk each check, mark it, and copy the results to Claude. Passing them all closes M2.

Test islands. Every link here opens a throwaway island that is never saved, so your own island is safe. Each check's green Set up link has it ready to play in under a minute; the plain links beside it show its other parts.

A season is 14 game days: autumn starts on day 28 and winter on day 42. &day=N plays the island from day 0 to day N while The island grows shows, a few seconds. A game hour is a minute at 1x, 15 seconds at 4x.

Visit, hint, home, flock. Each visitor comes once before its home exists. On a test island, Settings, Tests has Next morning and Skip a day: use Next morning to read the hint the visit leaves. Then the home link shapes that home for you (as Shaped, not found in doc 13 says, only ground you made counts) and the flock comes once the home has been right for 6 game hours, at its own morning hour (9:00 to 11:00). Those links open at 2:00 at night so the flock comes the same morning at 4x, about two minutes of watching.
