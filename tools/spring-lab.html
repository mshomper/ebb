Spring lab

Four weeks of spring and early summer at the pond, run by the game's own hourly step (src/world/creatures/spring.js): when happy frogs lay, when the spawn hatches, how many tadpoles the minnows eat, when the froglets leave, and which evenings the toad walks. Numbers are in recipes/species/frogspawn.json and toad.json. Open it beside the game to tune them.
