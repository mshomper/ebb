Gaps

Every ground has at least five takes. Two are weaker than the rest:

  • Rock and stone: no clean outdoor take on bare rock in soft shoes. Most are boots, a concrete slab or a patio. Softening a boot take, or a sand step layered with a soft tap, may serve.
  • Shallow water: mostly river or creek takes with flowing water under the steps. The game's own water sounds play around you, so the steps only need the splash; pick the ones whose flow is quietest.
