Loudness layers

Each piano was recorded at a few loudnesses. In between, two recordings are blended so a slow crescendo has no steps. The bands show which recordings sound at each loudness; the dot follows the test as it plays.

Mix sounds both recordings together through a blend. Pick sounds one of them each time, chosen with the blend's odds, which avoids the two strikes partly cancelling. Mix measured smoothest on most pianos, Pick on the Yamaha upright.

Blend curve (for Mix): toward 0 suits recordings that are in phase, toward 1 suits unrelated ones. Two recordings of one key sit between. Play the close-up and move it until the blends don't swell or sag.

Tuning

Each dot is one recorded note, measured against equal temperament at A = 440 Hz, in cents (a hundredth of a semitone). The band is the 3-cent target. Pianos are tuned with a slight stretch on purpose: the bass a little flat, the treble a little sharp, so their octaves sound pure. Choose how the game should tune them, then listen. Tap a dot to hear that note against a pure reference tone.

Download and memory budget

The game's budget is about 4 MB to download for the core piano and pad, and under 48 MB decoded on desktop (20 MB on phones). A full three-layer piano is far over that. Try these and keep what you can't hear the difference in.
