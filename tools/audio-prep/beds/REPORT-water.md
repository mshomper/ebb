# Water beds: what was made

Every take: 48 kHz stereo, high-pass 40 Hz, looped with a 2 s equal-power seam, -30 LUFS (trickle -33, drizzle -34), true peak at or below -3.3 dBFS, Opus 56 kbps plus AAC 64 kbps. Opus total 4.6 MB.

- **Sea**, takes a (46.3 s) and b (51.5 s).
  - a: slow stony waves 2:19.85 to 3:08.15, eight even breaks, seam in a trough. One sharp slap on stones near 0:43 comes round every loop.
  - b: calm sea 0:03.55 to 0:57.05, with an engine hum (79, 54 and 158 Hz) notched out. Its troughs are shallow, so this is the weaker seam.
  - Both sit over the Prince Edward Island distant surf at -8 dB. The surf loops on its own, offset half a loop from the waves.
- **Pond lap**: quiet sections of Lake Pedder only (anything 18 dB over the floor cut), joined in quiet gaps. A 9.37 kHz and 18.7 kHz whine notched out. A light peak limit (at most 4.4 dB) on the bigger laps.
  - Splashes kept for later frog one-shots (source times): big at 0:36.8, 0:49.2, 1:18.55 and 1:19.55; medium at 0:20.55, 0:21.7, 0:26.6, 0:34.9, 0:43.5, 0:54.8, 1:04.25, 1:04.85, 1:22.8 and 1:27.25.
- **Trickle**: the Duns stream has birds throughout.
  - The densest calls (0:15.4 to 0:24.4) are skipped.
  - Elsewhere, gentle spectral attenuation above 1.3 kHz pulls the calls 4 to 8 dB down; short water ticks are kept.
  - A faint warble trace near 3.5 kHz may remain (around 22 to 23 s in take a).
- **Stream run**: Springbrook's 30 s, cut into 3 to 6 s pieces at slightly different speeds with left and right alternating. It is clean.
- **Rain on water**: the balcony rain. Building thumps (0:22.5, 0:25.6) and an engine from 0:37 on are cut, leaving 32 s of material.
- **Rain on lily pads**: the raspberry leaves (24 s).
  - A rumble at 0:03.5 and a squeak at 0:11.6 are skipped; a 16.6 kHz tone and a 104 Hz hum are notched out.
  - The drops are peaky, and the limiter takes up to 7.7 dB off the loudest. If they sound softened, ship it unlimited at -34 LUFS and raise it in the mixer.
- **Rain on grass and reeds**: the campground rain.
  - A 75 to 95 Hz generator hum is removed by a 150 Hz high-pass.
  - Faint chirps (1:23.8, 1:29.8, 1:32.7, 1:42.5, 2:19.7) and bumps from 2:14 on are skipped. No voices.
- **Drizzle**: the raspberry leaves again, in a different order and at different speeds, high shelf -3 dB at 5 kHz, at -34 LUFS.
- **Not used yet**:
  - Atlantic at Pénestin: clean stretches 3:20 to 4:35, 4:50 to 7:05 and 7:25 to 9:20, for longer crash variety.
  - Sea cave: calm 0:52 to 1:58, with booms from 0:20 to 0:51 for cliffs.
  - Izmir gulls: clearest calls 0:00.5 to 0:03 and 0:04.3 to 0:08.3.
