ebb frog lab

The real island with frogs on it (S6b, docs 11, 12): pick a time of day and watch them rest, forage, call and swim. The lab shows the chorus call by call, checks that no two calls start within the gap, counts hops and swims, and measures what moving and drawing the frogs costs. The time buttons change only what is shown, never the world. All tools
