music render

Grows a piece exactly as the game would, plays it through the game's music sound (room, brightness, limiter) and makes a WAV file you can keep or share. The silence at both ends is trimmed, leaving about 3 seconds of the room after the last note, and the level is set to -18 LUFS with peaks under -1.5 dBFS.
