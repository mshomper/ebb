Made in code, and waiting for recordings

The sea, pond, streams, reeds, grass, open wind, winter air and every rain bed now play recordings from your Freesound picks (in assets/audio/beds, credited in assets/audio/CREDITS.md); a rain bed only starts when the rain slider is up. Each row says whether it plays a recording or the made-in-code stand-in. The small sounds, frogs and midges stay made in code for now.

  • The sea is shaped noise with slow waves until a distant surf recording lands.
  • Wind in reeds and grass is the open wind through a different color for each season, with a rustle on top.
  • Rain on each surface is shaped noise with drops; autumn reeds and grass crackle.
  • Distant visitors (a far gull, geese passing, an owl) need recordings and are not in this pass.
