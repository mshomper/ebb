ebb turtle lab

The European pond turtle at its drawn size, one view per motion, each looping (M2 turtle life). Every pose comes from the game's own rig and every number from recipes/species/turtle.json, so what moves here is what moves on the island. The line under each view says what the turtle is doing at that moment. Click a view to see it big; click again to put it back. Baked shell colours shows the shell as it looked before the plates were drawn per pixel, for comparison. The grid on the floor is 1 cm; the water is see-through so you can watch the strokes underneath. All tools
