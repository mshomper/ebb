# Camp model credits

Web copies for the keeper's camp (docs/future/13), made by `tools/assets/build-models.mjs` from the
entries in `tools/assets/models.json`. Matt's own models (the tent, fire, benches and the rest) need no
credit; the three clay pots below come from Sketchfab under CC-BY-4.0, and three more models from Poly Haven under CC0.

Each pot is changed for ebb the same way: its parts joined into one mesh, second UV set and tangents
dropped, thinned to 4,000 triangles, its maps resized to 512 px and saved as WebP, set base down at the
origin and scaled to its real size.

## Cooking pot: clay cooking pot

This work is based on "clay cooking pot" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/clay-cooking-pot-38c0785f24104cc0a1ffb91509ed4d12) by josemtavares (https://sketchfab.com/josemtavares) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

- Changes: the clay stove it sat on and the stove's grate left out (docs/future/13 has no stove), the pot and its lid joined, maps resized to 512 px WebP, scaled to 0.30 m along its longest side.
- Files: `cooking-pot.glb`

## Tea pot: Clay teapot

This work is based on "Clay teapot" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/clay-teapot-65e455e165d94b1aba4117a78093ae3e) by patrakeevasveta (https://sketchfab.com/patrakeevasveta) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

- Changes: simplified to 4,000 triangles, maps resized to 512 px WebP, scaled to 0.18 m along its longest side.
- Files: `tea-pot.glb`

## Dye pot: GENED 1105 Clay Pot

This work is based on "GENED 1105 Clay Pot" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gened-1105-clay-pot-c5332e6fcb7246dca817f53c4d8cddd5) by andrew_ogundimu (https://sketchfab.com/andrew_ogundimu) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

- Changes: faceted scan normals dropped and made again smooth, simplified to 4,000 triangles, color map resized to 512 px WebP, scaled to 0.32 m tall. Drawn as a dye pot.
- Files: `dye-pot.glb`

## Willow basket, larger pot and clay cup: Poly Haven (CC0)

Three models from Poly Haven under CC0 (no credit needed; listed here so the source is known), picked by Matt on October 24, 2026. Each is changed for ebb the same way as the pots: parts joined into one mesh, maps resized to 512 px and saved as WebP, set base down at the origin and scaled to its job's size.

- **Willow basket:** "Wicker Basket 02" by Kuutti Siitonen, https://polyhaven.com/a/wicker_basket_02, CC0. Its lid moved from beside the basket onto it, simplified to 8,000 triangles, scaled to 0.30 m tall. Files: `willow-basket.glb`
- **Larger pot:** "Ceramic Pot" by Aron Łyczek, https://polyhaven.com/a/ceramic_pot, CC0. Scaled to 0.40 m along its longest side. Files: `larger-pot.glb`
- **Clay cup:** "Planter Pot Clay" by Amal Kumar, https://polyhaven.com/a/planter_pot_clay, CC0. Simplified to 1,500 triangles, scaled to 0.09 m tall. Files: `clay-cup.glb`
