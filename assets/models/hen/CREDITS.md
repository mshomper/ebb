This work is based on "Chicken Rig" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/chicken-rig-b92464211274439b955700e3aa5c321f) by FourthGreen (https://sketchfab.com/FourthGreen), licensed under CC-BY-NC-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

- Changes: none to the file. The game folds its 50 joints to 17 bones (the toe joints into each foot, the second spine and third tail joint into their neighbours), cuts its blended feather edges to a hard edge, and moves it with its own code clips; only its Idle clip is used as made (`src/world/hen/rig.js`).
