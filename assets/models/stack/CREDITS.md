# The stack's cave mouth

- **Source:** Matt's model, `Ebb_Cave+Mouth.obj` (November 2, 2026), kept untouched in `Documents\Ebb\_assets`. 48,151 vertices, 96,302 triangles, no UVs.
- **The game's copy** (`cave-mouth.bin`, `cave-mouth-footprint.json`): made by `tools/assets/build-cave-mouth.mjs` from `recipes/setpieces/stack.json` `mouthModel`. Turned so its opening faces +z, scaled so the opening stands 2.3 m tall (6.3 x 5.2 x 7.5 m in all), welded and thinned to 14,000 triangles (Matt, November 2, 2026: decimating is fine; meshoptimizer, error 0.0008), with its ambient occlusion baked per vertex and over its chamber floor, and its solid footprint at knee height.
