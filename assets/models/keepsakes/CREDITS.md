# Keepsake artifact credits

Nine small artifact models for the keepsakes (docs/future/12), baked for ebb by `tools/bake-find-model.mjs` with `--size`: textures
sampled into linear vertex colors, UVs and materials dropped, parts joined, simplified, and rescaled to a real hand size
(the largest extent in meters, centred, base on y = 0). All nine are licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), so credit is required and is given below. Listed in
`recipes/world/keepsake-artifacts.json`; `tools/artifact-models.html` shows them with these credit lines.

## Old bullet: Artillery Shell

This work is based on "Artillery Shell" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/artillery-shell-ea28a6a5dc554a6a92653a80b9acca34) by SomeGuyUsingBlender (https://sketchfab.com/SomeGuyUsingBlender) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Changes: simplified from 4,920 to 1,372 triangles, rescaled to a bullet 3.5 cm long, and recolored (darkened and weathered toward a worn green-brown patina, with seeded noise so it bakes the same way every time).
- Files: `old-bullet.gltf`, `old-bullet.bin`

## Carved piece: Chinese Artifact 01
