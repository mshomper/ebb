# Instrument sample credits

Every instrument sample under `assets/audio/instruments/` comes from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios LLC, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, which is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). In the library's own words: "you can do whatever you want with these sounds (even make commercial software), no royalties, no credit, no special terms." We credit it anyway. The files were taken from the library at commit `c1ea7bcc3c73` (14 January 2026), checked one by one, and edited only as listed below: the start trimmed to 3 ms before the note's onset, the tail faded to true silence, and the result encoded to Ogg Opus (48 kHz) and AAC in .m4a. No file's gain was changed. Per-file measurements (level, brightness, length, trim, tuning) and every problem found are in `recipes/music/instruments/<id>.json`.

Matt chose the Steinway B grand on October 19, 2026; the Kawai grand, the Yamaha and Knight uprights and the sampled TX81Z FM piano were compared on the bench and then removed. Only the Knight's pedal recordings stay, played under the Steinway's pedal. The Steinway's game set (shorter tails, mono below C3, with AAC copies) replaces the comparison files once its download budget is settled.
