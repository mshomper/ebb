# Instrument sample credits

Every instrument sample under `assets/audio/instruments/` comes from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios LLC, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, which is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). In the library's own words: "you can do whatever you want with these sounds (even make commercial software), no royalties, no credit, no special terms." We credit it anyway. The files were taken from the library at commit `c1ea7bcc3c73` (14 January 2026), checked one by one, and edited only as listed below: the start trimmed to 3 ms before the note's onset, the tail faded to true silence, and the result encoded to Ogg Opus (48 kHz) and AAC in .m4a. No file's gain was changed. Per-file measurements (level, brightness, length, trim, tuning) and every problem found are in `recipes/music/instruments/<id>.json`.

Matt chose the Steinway B grand on October 19, 2026; the Kawai grand, the Yamaha and Knight uprights and the sampled TX81Z FM piano were compared on the bench and then removed. Only the Knight's pedal recordings stay, played under the Steinway's pedal. The Steinway files here are its game set (October 19, 2026): the middle five octaves (A#1 to D7), tails cut to 4 s with a 250 ms equal-power fade, notes below C3 folded to mono, encoded Opus at 90 kbps stereo and 60 kbps mono and AAC at 128 and 80 kbps. The edits listed in the table below were made first. The phone set in `piano-steinway-b/mobile/` (October 19, 2026) is made from the same game-set recordings (VCSL, CC0 1.0): 17 zones every major third (A#1 to D7), two velocity layers (pedal set v1 and v3, no-pedal set v2 and v3), mono from the left close microphone, the loud layer shifted by at most 1 ms to line up with the soft one, tails cut to 2.5 s with the same fade, no key-off sounds, and one Knight pedal press and lift; encoded Opus 60 kbps and AAC 80 kbps mono.
