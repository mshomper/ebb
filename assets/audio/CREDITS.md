# Audio credits

Every recording in the game, where it came from, its license and what was done to it (doc 25, "Processing"). Instrument samples are listed in `instruments/CREDITS.md`.

All files below are CC0 1.0 (public domain) unless marked otherwise. The one exception, approved by Matt on October 19, 2026 because no CC0 reed-bed recording exists, is the YleArkisto reed bed (CC BY 4.0): it is credited here and in the game's credits, and listed in `LICENSES-NONCC0.md`.

Each bed is a looping take made from the stretch named, encoded as Ogg Opus 56 kbps stereo with an AAC (.m4a) copy for older Safari. Source WAVs are not in the repository.
