# Finds on the shore

Five small shore lives to find by tapping (docs/future/11). Where they live comes from how you shape the coast. Nothing about them is saved; the journal keeps what you have found under **Living things > Small lives**, which lists only what you have found.

| Find | Where | What it does | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beadlet anemone | Rock pools (a hollow of rock that reaches the tide band) | Open under water; closes to a dome at low tide or when you come close | "Low-Poly Anemone" by Ben Lebreux, CC-BY-4.0 |
| Periwinkle | On the wrack | Groups creep a little each tide | CC0 shell scan, cut to 900 triangles |
| Lugworm casts | Wet sand and mud the tide has left | Laid fresh after each tide, with a burrow dip beside each | Drawn into the sand by the terrain shader, no mesh |
| Hermit crab | Rock pools | Creeps about the pool floor under water; stops when you come close or the pool drains | CC0 model, cut to 1,487 triangles |
| Young edible crab | In the wrack and the pools | Runs sideways away from you, then settles back | "Crab 3d Model Free" by iRahulRajput, CC-BY-4.0 |

Credits for both CC-BY models are in README.md and `assets/models/finds/CREDITS.md`. `tools/finds-models.html` shows every model at true scale.

## Click-test

1. Open the island and paint rock along the shore in the tide band, then lower a hollow in it. When the tide has filled it, red-pink anemones sit open in the pool, and often a hermit crab in an orange shell.
2. Zoom right in on an anemone: it closes to a dome. Pull back: it opens again. At low tide it stays closed.
3. Watch the hermit crab for a few seconds from a little way off: it picks slowly about the pool. Zoom close: it stops.
4. Look along the weed on the rocks: small grey-brown periwinkles in groups, now and then a small red crab. Zoom in near a crab: it runs sideways away, then settles.
5. At low tide, look along the wet sand: coils of sand, each with a small dip beside it. After the next tide they are in new places.
6. Tap any of them: a card names it. Open the journal, Living things: Small lives shows a mark and a row for each kind you found, with its page and sketch.
7. On your phone, check that the Small lives list and the find pages fit with no sideways scroll.
8. Open tools/finds-models.html and drag the models around.
